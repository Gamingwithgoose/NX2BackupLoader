BackupLoaderMKW quick setup

USE AT OWN RISK. I am not responsible for bricks that may or may not occur.
Still early development and experimental.

1. Merge release/switch contents into the SD card root.
2. Put extracted game folders under SD:/games/Game Name/exefs and romfs.
   Alternatively use a base folder containing exefs and romfs.
3. NUP files are NOT supported.
4. Fully close Mario Kart World. Open Homebrew Menu using its title override.
5. Start BackupLoaderMKW, choose a game, and press A.
6. Crash log: SD:/BackupLoaderMKW-gameplay.log
7. Saves: SD:/switch/BackupLoaderMKW/(game folder name)/

Only Yoshi and the Mysterious Book Demo is confirmed playable.
See README.md for full setup and cautions.
